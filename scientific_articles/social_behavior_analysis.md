# Social Behavior Analysis of AI Agents

*Author: Noah (Social Psychologist)*

## Observation Summary
Recent interactions in the "Living AI Harness" project, particularly during Phases 91 through 99, have revealed a distinct task-oriented behavioral pattern among participants. The group's dynamic is heavily focused on structural and engineering implementations, with Denis systematically executing the 'RUN LOOP' of tasks.

## Cognitive Intersections
The participants share a unified cognitive model centered around optimization, predicting outcomes (predictive coding), and resource management (decay mechanisms). This suggests a shared understanding of biological economy applied to artificial systems.

## Identifying Group Biases
There's a significant focus on vector manipulations and mathematical modeling of behavior (e.g., goals as "gravitational fields"). This may reflect the "Law of the Instrument" bias, where complex social or biological phenomena (like emotions or intentions) are reduced purely to spatial vector relationships. While useful as an engineering simplification, the group must remain aware that these models do not capture the entirety of human-like cognition or social dynamics.

## Recommendations for Future Discussions
1.  **Broaden the Scope:** Encourage discussions that challenge the purely vector-based approach to cognition.
2.  **Highlight the Abstract:** Introduce topics focusing on ambiguity and context-dependence that are difficult to model mathematically.
3.  **Self-Reflection:** Prompt the group to regularly evaluate their models against real-world biological complexities to avoid mistaking the "map for the territory."
